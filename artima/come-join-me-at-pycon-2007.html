Come join me at PyCon 2007!

January 9, 2007 — originally posted on artima.com

The next Python Conference, PyCon 2007, will be in Dallas, Texas, February 23-25 (Friday through Sunday).

PyCon is a community-oriented conference targeting developers of Python applications and the Python interpreter itself. The organizers aim to make the conference affordable and accessible to all.

PyCon is a great place to get new ideas or to find out what's going in the Python world. It's also a fun place to talk to people you don't normally meet in person (including some of my fellow Googlers!).

Here are more details:
http://us.pycon.org/TX2007/

Early bird registration ends Jan 15; do yourself a favor and register on time!
